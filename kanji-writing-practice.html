Sync across devices

Progress syncs through a private GitHub Gist, manually — nothing pushes or pulls on its own. On your first device, paste a token and leave the Gist ID blank, then click Push — that creates the gist and fills in the ID here. Paste that same ID (with its own token) into this field on every other device, then Pull there to bring the deck over.

Push sends this device's progress to GitHub, overwriting what's there. Pull brings GitHub's copy here, overwriting this device's. Each one checks the other side's save time first and asks you to confirm before overwriting anything that looks newer — so it's on you to pick the right direction, not a timestamp race.
